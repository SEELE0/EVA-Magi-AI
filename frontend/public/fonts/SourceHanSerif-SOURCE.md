`SourceHanSerifSC-SemiBold.woff2` is the official Simplified Chinese SemiBold
(weight 600) font converted from OTF to WOFF2 with fontTools 4.60.2 and
Brotli 1.2.0. All glyphs and OpenType layout tables are retained; this is
format compression, with no glyph subsetting or design changes. The full
character set supports user-edited prompts rather than only built-in text.

`SourceHanSerifSC-Medium.woff2` is the official Simplified Chinese Medium
(weight 500) font converted with the same tools and without glyph subsetting.
- Official source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif/release/OTF/SimplifiedChinese/SourceHanSerifSC-Medium.otf
- Downloaded: 2026-10-04
- Original SHA-256: `1d4dc4b757c07034e2412d6edf48f54f94ec7172d4deb3b90a3e4fc9dcb94f5d`

Medium is used for Chinese role-prompt textareas in the shared node
configuration dialog. SemiBold is used for the Chinese execute-decision button
and the Chinese restore/save/cancel actions in the setting-book dialog.
Japanese and English retain their existing fonts.
